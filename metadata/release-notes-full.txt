Full release.

Included: local reading, collections, tags, ratings, history, the download queue,
OPDS 1.2 and 2.0 browse, Komga and Kavita browse, and Komga progress sync.
Kavita progress is read-only. librarian has no content or progress protocol.

Not included in this revision: a checked emulator widget, a Pdfium-from-source
APK, and two matching Nix or F-Droid APK payloads. Compare those with
scripts/apk-payload.sh after META-INF signing entries are excluded.
The optional rar archive feature is not part of the F-Droid build.
